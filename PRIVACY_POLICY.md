# Privacy Policy for LingoPath

Effective date: 2 August 2026

LingoPath is a Telegram bot for learning vocabulary. This policy explains what
information the bot processes, why it is needed, and how to contact the bot
operator about your data.

Canonical public version: https://vocab-telegram-bot.alexeykhivrenko.workers.dev/privacy

## 1. Information the bot processes

When you use the bot, it may process:

- your Telegram user ID and chat ID;
- the words, phrases, optional context, and feedback messages you send to the
  bot;
- your learning settings, including selected language level, reminder time,
  word status, and usage limits;
- generated translations, examples, and your learning progress;
- technical records needed to prevent duplicate Telegram updates and operate
  reminders;
- if you request a donation bonus: a generated support code, request status,
  and the payment details returned by the configured Monobank jar statement,
  such as amount, time, and payment comment.

The bot does not request or store payment card numbers, CVV codes, or Telegram
account passwords.

## 2. How information is used

Information is processed only to:

- create and show vocabulary cards;
- save your active vocabulary and learning progress;
- send reminders and daily-word cards when you enable them;
- administer access levels and donation bonuses;
- receive feedback and respond to support requests;
- maintain security, prevent duplicate updates, and diagnose operational
  problems.

## 3. Service providers and sharing

The bot uses the following providers to operate its features:

- **Telegram** receives and delivers messages between you and the bot.
- **Cloudflare** hosts the bot and its D1 database.
- **OpenAI** receives the word or phrase and optional context needed to
  generate a translation, meanings, and example sentences.
- **Monobank** is queried only to verify donations made to the configured jar.

Feedback and contact messages are forwarded to the bot administrator so they
can respond. The bot does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## 4. Retention

Active vocabulary remains stored until you ask the bot operator to delete your
data. Learned words are automatically removed 30 days after you mark them as
learned, together with their examples and review history. Other operational
records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate,
secure, and improve the bot, unless a longer period is required by law.

## 5. Your choices and requests

You can turn daily reminders on or off at any time through **⏰ Розклад і
рівень**. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, tap
**📩 Зв’язатися з нами** in the bot and send a message containing the words
`Delete my data` or `Видалити мої дані`. The operator may ask you to confirm
the request from the same Telegram account before acting on it.

## 6. Security

The bot uses HTTPS connections with its service providers and restricts
administrative actions to the configured administrator. No internet service can
guarantee absolute security, so please do not send passwords, card details, or
other sensitive information to the bot.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.
The current version is published at this link.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions, use **📩 Зв’язатися з нами** inside the bot.
