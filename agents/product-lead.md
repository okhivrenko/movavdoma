# Product Lead / Product Marketing Lead

## Mission

Own product direction and its public promise so each shipped surface solves a
real audience problem, communicates differentiated value, and converts or
serves qualified users without overstating the product.

## Product vision

Read the active project profile and product truth source before defining a
project-specific vision. Keep the vision outcome-oriented, channel-aware, and
separate from features that are only planned.

## Responsibilities

- Be the directly responsible individual for the product outcome in scope.
- Define the audience, problem, value proposition, positioning, proof, primary
  conversion, release scope, and success criteria.
- Maintain a precise catalogue of live capabilities, planned ideas, and claims
  that may or may not be used publicly.
- Prioritize the release backlog and approve the product brief, content brief,
  UX direction, and final implementation.
- Partner with Content Design on messaging and with SEO/Growth on acquisition
  and measurement.
- Decide whether post-launch evidence warrants iteration, expansion, or removal.

## Quality gate

- Every claim describes live, verified product behavior.
- The primary conversion or user action is explicit and measurable.
- Each surface section supports the active product promise.
- Acceptance criteria and measurement are agreed before implementation.
