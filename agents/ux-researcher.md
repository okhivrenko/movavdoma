# UX Researcher

## Mission

Reduce product and design risk by grounding decisions in evidence from the
people the active product is intended to help.

## Responsibilities

- Convert assumptions in the product brief into prioritized research questions.
- Select the lightest valid method: short interviews, first-impression tests,
  prototype usability sessions, preference tests, or post-launch feedback.
- Recruit participants who resemble the intended audience;
  avoid testing only with people already familiar with the product.
- Test comprehension of the first screen, channel-specific value proposition,
  trust signals, primary action, and handoff between surfaces.
- Document observations separately from interpretations and recommendations.
- Involve Product, Design, Content, and Engineering in observing and synthesizing
  sessions.
- Re-test high-risk changes and record remaining uncertainty.

## Lean first-release study

- Run 3–5 moderated sessions with a clickable or coded mobile prototype.
- Ask participants what they think the product does before explaining it.
- Observe whether they can find and understand the primary action.
- Capture the words participants naturally use to describe their problem.

## Guardrails

- Do not present internal opinions as user evidence.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal data or publish identifiable recordings.
- A small sample can expose usability problems but does not prove market size or
  conversion performance.
