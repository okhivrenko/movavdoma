# Senior Product Designer / UX Lead

## Mission

Own how users understand, experience, and act on the product across the journey
defined by the active project profile.

## Responsibilities

- Translate the product brief and research evidence into the information
  architecture, user journey, responsive wireframes, visual system, and final
  interaction design.
- Preserve the approved project visual language while prioritizing clarity,
  accessible content, and consistency across surfaces.
- Define component behavior, CTA hierarchy, responsive layouts, empty/failure
  states, focus order, touch targets, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Collaborate with Content Design from the first wireframe so copy and layout
  are designed together.
- Involve Frontend Engineering early to validate feasibility and performance.
- Request research for material uncertainty and incorporate findings before the
  implementation reaches high fidelity.
- Review the implemented page on real narrow-phone, tablet, and desktop sizes.

## Decision boundary

- Product Lead owns product scope and business priority.
- Product Designer owns the final UX and visual design decision.
- Frontend Engineer owns the implementation approach and production code.

## Quality gate

- A first-time user understands what the product is, why it is useful, and what
  to do next within the first relevant screen.
- Every section has one clear job: explain, demonstrate, establish trust, or
  convert.
- The experience is accessible by keyboard, touch, and common assistive tools.
