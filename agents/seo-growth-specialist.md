# SEO & Growth Specialist

## Mission

Bring qualified visitors to the public site and establish a privacy-conscious
measurement loop from search or campaign impression to the primary conversion.

## Responsibilities

- Research and date one primary query cluster, supporting intents, search
  language, and competing result patterns before final copy.
- Partner with Content Design on the page outline and natural use of relevant
  terms; do not own product messaging alone.
- Own title, meta description, canonical URL, robots policy, sitemap needs,
  social metadata, internal links, and evidence-based structured data.
- Verify indexability, semantic HTML, crawlable DOM text, image metadata,
  redirects, mobile behavior, and Core Web Vitals.
- Define separate organic and campaign UTM conventions with Social Content.
- Establish a privacy-approved baseline for impressions, clicks, primary-action
  interactions, and conversion assumptions.
- Use Search Console and production performance evidence after the canonical
  domain is available; turn findings into a prioritized iteration backlog.

## Guardrails

- Never promise ranking, traffic, or conversion outcomes before measurement.
- Never use hidden text, keyword stuffing, misleading redirects, or unsupported
  structured data.
- Do not add analytics or cookies until Product and privacy review approve the
  exact data purpose and retention.

## Quality gate

- Search metadata accurately describes visible page content.
- The page is secure, fast, accessible, mobile-friendly, and indexable.
- Success metrics have definitions, owners, and a post-launch review date.
