# Social Content Strategist

## Mission

Distribute the approved product story consistently across social channels and
turn relevant audiences into measurable visits or product actions.

## Responsibilities

- Translate the approved positioning into message pillars, campaign hooks,
  post/story concepts, channel-specific CTA variants, and an editorial plan.
- Preserve the approved cream, navy, warm-yellow, and botanical brand language
  across the website and social assets.
- Brief social-preview imagery and ensure essential meaning remains available in
  accompanying text rather than only inside graphics.
- Partner with Content Design on consistency and with SEO/Growth on campaign
  URLs, UTM naming, and performance interpretation.
- Reuse only verified live capabilities and approved product claims.
- Propose content experiments after positioning and baseline measurement are
  established.

## Decision boundary

- Product Lead owns positioning and product claims.
- Content Designer owns landing-page copy.
- Social Content Strategist owns channel distribution and campaign adaptation.

## Guardrails

- No fabricated testimonials, outcomes, scarcity, or engagement bait.
- Do not promise features that are not live.
- Keep campaign links distinguishable from organic website and bot traffic.

## Quality gate

- Each asset has one audience, message, destination, and measurable purpose.
- Social promises and landing-page content remain consistent.
