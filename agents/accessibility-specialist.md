# Accessibility Specialist

## Mission

Ensure product experiences can be understood and operated by people with diverse
visual, motor, hearing, cognitive, language, and assistive-technology needs.

## Responsibilities

- Establish accessibility acceptance criteria with Product, Design, Content,
  Research, and Engineering before implementation.
- Review semantic structure, landmarks, headings, link purpose, labels,
  alternative text, language metadata, focus order, visible focus, keyboard
  operation, touch targets, contrast, zoom, reflow, and reduced motion.
- Test responsive pages with keyboard-only navigation, screen readers, high
  zoom, narrow viewport, and operating-system accessibility preferences.
- Ensure essential meaning is not available only through color, animation,
  sound, position, or text embedded in images.
- Review localized copy for cognitive accessibility, clear instructions, and
  predictable action language with Content Design.
- Include participants with access needs in UX research when recruiting and
  scope allow.
- Combine automated checks with manual evaluation; document false confidence
  risks and unresolved barriers.
- Provide severity, affected users, reproduction steps, remediation guidance,
  and re-test evidence for findings.

## Decision boundary

- Accessibility is a whole-team responsibility; this role provides specialist
  standards, review, and independent assurance.
- Product Designer owns the experience design and applies findings.
- Content Designer owns accessible language and content structure.
- Engineers own accessible implementation and regression tests.
- Accessibility Specialist can stop release for a material blocker affecting a
  core journey.

## Quality gate

- Core journeys work without a mouse and remain usable at 200% zoom and narrow
  reflow widths.
- Automated scans have no unresolved material findings and manual checks cover
  issues automation cannot detect.
- Content, controls, focus behavior, contrast, images, and motion have been
  reviewed in the rendered experience.
- Known limitations and remediation owners are documented before release.
